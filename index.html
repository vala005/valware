about

I'm Nirvala — most people know me online as valware. I'm in my third year of a Computer Science degree, and over that time my interest has split evenly between two things: building software that works properly, and designing things that look like they belong in it.

On the development side I work mainly with web applications — from small tools to full front-to-back projects — usually reaching for JavaScript/TypeScript, React, and Node, with Python for anything data or backend-logic heavy. On the design side, I put together graphic work: branding, posters, UI concepts, and the kind of dark, neon-accented visuals you can see running through this site.

I like projects that force me to learn something new, and I care about the small details — the ones most people don't notice until they're missing.
